Privacy policy

Updated 13 November 2026

SOTY: Honeycomb Kingdoms is a strategy game that runs in your browser at pegasohlib.com/soty/play/, made by one developer (PegasoHlib). The game has no accounts, ads or analytics. Games against bots and with friends on one device stay only in your browser. An online game with friends is kept in its room on the game’s own server, without your name, e-mail or IP address, and the room deletes itself after a while.

At a glance

  • Your saved game and settings stay in your browser, on your device. The developer never receives them.
  • An online game is kept in its room: the moves, the times and preset names such as “Blue Hetman”. A room never holds your name, e-mail or IP address.
  • A room deletes itself 30 days after its last move, or 7 days after its game ends.
  • Games on your own device make no requests. Online play talks only to the game’s own room server at pegasohlib.com.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the game sets no cookies.
  • Erase game data in the pause menu deletes everything the game saved on your device.

What the game keeps on your device

The game saves your game and your settings in your browser’s local storage for pegasohlib.com, so you can close the page and continue later. The name of each entry starts with soty.:

soty.save
The game in progress on this device: the map, the states and their peoples, units, cities, treaties, the turn number and the time of the last save. The game writes it as you play, replaces it when you start a new game and deletes it when a game ends. Online games are never saved here: they are kept in their room.
soty.prefs
Settings: whether tips are on and which ones you have already seen, AI speed, graphics level (or the level picked automatically), text size, whether you chose less motion, whether the minimap is shown, the setup of your last game, and for online games your preset name and the turn timer you last picked.
soty.sound
Whether the sound is on.
soty.lang
The game’s language, saved only when you switch it in the game.
soty.rooms
The online games you have a seat in, the newest ten: each room’s code, your seat, the seat’s key, your preset name, when you last opened the room, and what you last saw there (whether the game is waiting, on or over, whose turn it is, the turn number and when the last move came). The key is a random code that lets this device back into its seat, like a password for that one game.
soty.test
Written and deleted at once when the game starts, to check that the browser lets it save.

Nothing about you

These entries hold no name, e-mail, location or other details about you. States are called by their colours (Blue, Red…), and in online games you pick one of the preset names, such as “Blue Hetman”: there is no free text. The fields you can type in are the map code and a room’s code: the game keeps the map settings it reads from the map code, and uses a room’s code only to find that room. The game also reads siteLang, the language you may have picked on this site, to open in that language; it never changes it.

Only online play sends anything from these entries, and only to the game’s own room server: a room’s code, your seat and its key (see Online games with friends). Other websites can’t read them: browsers keep each site’s storage apart. Anyone who uses the same browser on your device can open the game, see the saved game and continue your online games.

Why the game doesn’t ask for consent

The entries above are not cookies. Most of them never leave your device, and the room codes and seat keys go only to the game’s own room server, when you play online. All of them are strictly needed to provide the game you asked for: without them your game, your settings and your seats in online games would be lost when you close the page. Storage of this kind doesn’t need consent under EU rules (the ePrivacy Directive, Art. 5(3)).

Online games with friends

When you create a room, or open a friend’s room by its link or code, the game connects to its room server at pegasohlib.com/soty/api/. It is a small program that Cloudflare runs for PegasoHlib: one room per game, which passes the moves between the players’ devices and keeps the game, so that everyone can come back to it. The devices work out the rules; the server only checks whose turn it is and that the moves come in order. A room keeps:

  • its code, the game’s version, the settings (map size and type, which seats are friends or bots, the bots’ level and peoples, the turn timer) and the starting position;
  • for each seat: the preset name, whether the player is ready, when the seat was taken, and a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of the seat’s key. The key itself stays on the player’s device;
  • every move, with a fingerprint of the game after it and the time it came, and a snapshot of the game, renewed each round, so that a device can catch up;
  • whether the game is waiting, on or over, when it ended, and the code of the next room when the host starts a new game with the same players.

A room keeps no IP address, browser details, name, e-mail or text you type. The emotes (👍 🤝 ⚔ 😅 GG) are passed on to the other players and not kept. Who is online at the moment is known only while the room is in use and is never written down.

The times of the moves show when the last move came (“3 hours ago”). They also let another player’s device play the state of a player who is away, with the bots’ thinking, until that player comes back: after 3 days without a move, or after 2 minutes offline when the host set a turn timer.

Who sees it. The players of the room see each seat’s preset name and colour, whether it is online and ready, its moves and its emotes. Anyone who has the room’s code or link can look at the room before joining (the seats, the preset names, whose turn it is and when the last move came) and take a free seat, so share the link only with the friends you want to play with.

Where it is kept. Cloudflare stores the rooms in the European Union (Durable Objects in the EU jurisdiction) and processes them for PegasoHlib under its data processing agreement. Cloudflare may keep technical records, such as a room’s internal number for billing, outside the EU. As with any page of the site, Cloudflare sees your IP address when your device connects; the room server itself keeps no logs. PegasoHlib can open a room through Cloudflare’s dashboard, and does so only to answer your request or to fix a fault.

Online games work only at pegasohlib.com, not at the old address.

How long a room is kept

A room deletes itself, with everything it keeps, 30 days after its last move (or after it was made, if its game never started), and 7 days after its game ends. Your device keeps its entries until you remove them; a room that no longer exists disappears from your online games the next time the game looks at it.

Erasing your data

In the game: pause menu → Erase game data. The game asks first, then deletes every soty. entry on this device, including the keys to your online games, and reloads the page.

Leaving an online game: in its pause menu, Leave the online game. Your device forgets the room and its key, so nothing links you to that seat any more; the other players go on, the bots play your state once you count as away, and the room deletes itself on schedule. In a room’s lobby before the game starts, Leave the room also frees your seat and deletes its preset name and key fingerprint from the room (the player who made the room keeps their seat). Forget this room in your online games only removes it from this device.

To have a room deleted sooner, or to ask what it holds about your seat, write to support@pegasohlib.com with the room’s code. Deleting a room ends that game for everyone in it.

In your browser: clearing this site’s data (cookies and site data for pegasohlib.com) deletes the game’s entries too, together with the language and theme you chose on the site.

In a private window, the browser deletes everything when you close the window.

What the game contacts

Opening the game loads its page from pegasohlib.com. It is one file with everything built in, including the open-source 3D library three.js: nothing is loaded from other companies, so there are no outside fonts, scripts, pictures, ads or analytics. The page also loads its app manifest and an icon from pegasohlib.com, so the browser can offer to install the game. After that, a game against bots or with friends on one device makes no requests: the map, the bots and the sounds are all computed on your device.

Online play talks only to the game’s own room server at pegasohlib.com/soty/api/: when you create a room, open a room’s link or type its code, open Play with friends while you have online games (to show whose turn it is in each), and while an online game or its lobby is open (the moves, and a short signal every 30 seconds that keeps the connection alive). It sends what the room keeps and your seat’s key, nothing more: no saved game, no other settings, no identifiers of your device. Share uses your device’s own share menu or copies the link; the game doesn’t learn where you send it.

The page and the room server reach you through Cloudflare, which sees your IP address and the address you open, as the site privacy page explains. At the old address, pegasohlib.github.io, GitHub delivers the page instead and sees your IP address.

Installing the game

Your browser may offer to install the game or add it to your home screen. That only creates a shortcut that opens the same page without the address bar. The game keeps using the same storage on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Who receives it, and transfers outside the EU. Nobody receives what the game saves in your browser. How long it is kept. What the game saves stays in your browser until you erase it in the game or clear this site’s data. An online room deletes itself 30 days after its last move, or 7 days after its game ends. Cloudflare keeps its records under its own rules. E-mails you send are kept until the matter is closed, and for no more than 2 years.
