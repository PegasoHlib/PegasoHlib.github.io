Privacy policy

Updated 12 November 2026

SOTY: Honeycomb Kingdoms is a strategy game that runs in your browser at pegasohlib.com/soty/play/, made by one developer (PegasoHlib). The game has no accounts, ads or analytics. It keeps your game only in your own browser and sends it nowhere. This policy covers the current version: games against bots and with friends on one device.

At a glance

  • Your saved game and settings stay in your browser, on your device. The developer never receives them.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the game sets no cookies.
  • While you play, the game makes no requests at all. Only the page itself loads from pegasohlib.com, like any page of the site.
  • Erase game data in the pause menu deletes everything the game saved on your device.
  • This version has no online play. Before it comes, this policy will change.

What the game keeps on your device

The game saves your game and your settings in your browser’s local storage for pegasohlib.com, so you can close the page and continue later. The name of each entry starts with soty.:

soty.save
The game in progress: the map, the states and their peoples, units, cities, treaties, the turn number and the time of the last save. The game writes it as you play, replaces it when you start a new game and deletes it when a game ends.
soty.prefs
Settings: whether tips are on and which ones you have already seen, AI speed, graphics level (or the level picked automatically), whether the minimap is shown, and the setup of your last game.
soty.sound
Whether the sound is on.
soty.lang
The game’s language, saved only when you switch it in the game.
soty.test
Written and deleted at once when the game starts, to check that the browser lets it save.

Nothing about you

These entries hold no name, e-mail, location or other details about you. States are called by their colours (Blue, Red…). The only field you can type in is the map code: the game keeps the map settings it reads from the code, not what you typed. The game also reads siteLang, the language you may have picked on this site, to open in that language; it never changes it.

The developer never receives any of this, and other websites can’t read it: browsers keep each site’s storage apart. Anyone who uses the same browser on your device can open the game and see the saved game.

Why the game doesn’t ask for consent

The entries above are not cookies and are never sent anywhere. They are strictly needed to provide the game you opened: without them your game and settings would be lost when you close the page. Storage of this kind doesn’t need consent under EU rules (the ePrivacy Directive, Art. 5(3)).

Erasing your data

In the game: pause menu → Erase game data. The game asks first, then deletes every soty. entry on this device and reloads the page.

In your browser: clearing this site’s data (cookies and site data for pegasohlib.com) deletes the game’s entries too, together with the language and theme you chose on the site.

In a private window, the browser deletes everything when you close the window.

What the game contacts

Opening the game loads its page from pegasohlib.com. It is one file with everything built in, including the open-source 3D library three.js: nothing is loaded from other companies, so there are no outside fonts, scripts, pictures, ads or analytics. The page also loads its app manifest and an icon from pegasohlib.com, so the browser can offer to install the game. After that the game makes no requests while you play: the map, the bots and the sounds are all computed on your device.

The page reaches you through Cloudflare, which sees your IP address and the page you open, as the site privacy page explains. At the old address, pegasohlib.github.io, GitHub delivers the page instead and sees your IP address. The game sends no data of its own: no saved game, no settings, no identifiers.

Installing the game

Your browser may offer to install the game or add it to your home screen. That only creates a shortcut that opens the same page without the address bar. The game keeps using the same storage on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Online play

This version has no online play. Games with friends by link are planned. Before they arrive, this policy will explain what an online game sends and keeps, and the date at the top will change.

Who receives it, and transfers outside the EU. Nobody receives what the game saves: it stays in your browser. Cloudflare delivers the game’s page and sees your IP address; at the old address, pegasohlib.github.io, GitHub does. E-mail you send passes through Cloudflare and is kept in a Gmail mailbox by Google, and Anthropic may process it when an AI assistant helps read it. Some of these companies are based in the United States or other countries outside the EU. Those transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the company takes part in it, or on the company’s standard contractual clauses.

How long it is kept. What the game saves stays in your browser until you erase it in the game or clear this site’s data. Cloudflare keeps its records under its own rules. E-mails you send are kept until the matter is closed, and for no more than 2 years.

Your rights. You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy to take elsewhere (portability), and you can object to processing or withdraw consent at any time. Write to the address above. You’ll get an answer within one month. Most of the data is only on your device, so you can usually do this yourself in the app.

Complaints. You can complain to the data protection authority of your country.

Age. You need to be at least 15 to use the app on your own. If you are younger, ask a parent or guardian to agree first.

Language. Translations of this policy are made with the help of AI. If a translation differs from the English text, the version more favourable to you applies.
