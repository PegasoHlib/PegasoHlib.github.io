Kolys

A letter to your future self

Write yourself a letter, add photos and seal it until the day you choose. Until then nobody can open it, not even you. When the time comes, the capsule opens by itself.

Download for Android

An app for Android phones

Write

A letter in your own words, with up to 10 photos. Gentle questions help when the page is blank.

Seal

In a month, a year, ten years or on any date. The date is checked online, so moving the phone’s clock won’t open it early.

Open

On the day a notification arrives, the capsule opens with a little light show, and the letter is yours to read.

Keep

Capsules are saved in a hidden folder on your own Google Drive. On a new phone, sign in with the same account and every capsule comes back.

Remember

One tap adds the opening day to your calendar, so the reminder finds you even years later on another phone.

Private by design

No accounts of ours, no servers, no ads, no tracking. Your letters stay on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive.
