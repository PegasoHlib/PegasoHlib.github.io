Khronometr

Screen time for all time

How long you have spent in every app and game — not for the last few weeks, but for all time. On your phone, tablet and PC together.

💻 Khronometr for Windows 🔒 Privacy Policy

🤖 Android app — coming to Google Play

What it can do

♾️

All-time history

Every app and game, day by day, kept forever — with records, titles and your longest streaks.

⏮️

History from before

On the first start it takes everything the phone still remembers, up to about two years back.

📱

All your devices

Phones, tablets and a Windows PC in one place, through a hidden folder on your own Google Drive.

⏳

Limits that work

Time per day, forbidden hours, a “Time’s up” screen — for one app, a category, your own group, on the devices you choose.

🧩

Widgets

Today, the week, the top apps, one app, your limits and all-time totals right on the home screen.

📶

Traffic

Wi-Fi and mobile data per app, for any days you pick.

How it works

1

Install on the phone

Give it “Usage access” once — it reads the history the phone keeps and counts from then on.

2

Add your other devices

Sign in with Google on each phone, tablet and the Windows app — they find each other.

3

Look back any time

Days, weeks, months or years, every app or a category, one device or all of them.

🔒Your data stays yours

No servers, no ads, no tracking. Everything is kept on your devices and, if you want, in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive.
