Privacy policy

Updated 6 November 2026

ZORYT Anime Training Simulator is a Roblox game made by one developer, PegasoHlib, and it is still in development. This page says in plain words what the game saves about you, where, and who can read it.

At a glance

  • The game runs on Roblox. Roblox gives the game your account number (id) and your names, and Roblox's own privacy policy governs your account.
  • The game never asks for your email, real name, phone number, address or payment details, and they do not reach the developer. Your email address reaches the developer only if you write yourself.
  • Your progress is saved on Roblox's servers, in the game's data stores that PegasoHlib manages.
  • The game sends Roblox events about how people play (for example, finishing the first training). They are tied to your account and are used to find hard or boring places.
  • If you send a report from the game's settings, it is saved with your account name, the place where you stood and your device type.
  • You can delete your account yourself in the game: SETTINGS → CHARACTER → DELETE ACCOUNT. You or your parent can also write to the developer's email.

What the game knows about you

ZORYT is played inside Roblox, so Roblox, not the developer, runs your account. When you join, Roblox tells the game your account id, your username and your display name, and the language of your device. The game uses them to find your progress, to show your name to other players, and to pick the language of the game's texts.

The game does not ask you to type your age, real name, email, address or phone number, and it has no way to see them. It contains no ads. It does not send anything to servers other than Roblox's own. When you give or trade a pet, the game reads from Roblox how old your account is: both sides need at least 1 day, to stop throwaway accounts. That age is not saved. When you join, the game reads from Roblox whether paid random items and trading paid items are allowed for your account, and keeps the answer only until you leave. While you play, the game also asks Roblox whether the players on your server are your friends and whether you are in the game's group, for the friends' and group bonuses. These answers are not saved.

Your saved progress

So that you can come back, the game saves your progress in a data store on Roblox, under your account id. PegasoHlib manages that data store and can read it. It holds:

  • strength, coins, swords, auras, pets, potions, titles, belts, quests, story choices and what you found in the Codex;
  • the furthest world you opened, your best tower floor, rebirths and boss results;
  • your settings: language, graphics level, music volume, size of menus, and where you moved the touch buttons;
  • your last place in the world, so the game can put you back;
  • the numbers about purchases (see Purchases below);
  • your season pass, play time, redeemed codes, daily rewards and gift limits;
  • the id of the player who invited you, only until the invite reward is paid and for 14 days at most; marks of invite rewards; and your house (guild) id, if you have one.

Other players can see only what the game itself shows: your name and numbers on a leaderboard, your pets and titles in the world, and your house.

A short list of testers and of people who got passes or gifts from the owner is kept by account id.

Boards, houses and trades

Leaderboards keep your account id and one number (such as Power or your best tower floor) and show your Roblox name, avatar and result to all players. The leader can stand as a statue in the lobby, and rare finds are announced in chat with the player's name.

A house of the Order of Sakura is a small team of players. Its record holds the house name and tag, the members' account ids and display names, their roles, fame points and last visit time, and join requests and invites. Other players can see this. House names and tags are checked by Roblox's text filter. The leader and advisors can write a short message for the house, checked by the same filter. Other players can read it.

Pet trades and team towers happen between players who are in the same game at that moment. A trade is not saved as a separate record.

Play events

The game sends Roblox's analytics service events that Roblox ties to your account. They are about play, not about you as a person:

  • steps of the first hour (for example, the intro finished, the first training, the first egg, the first rebirth);
  • coins earned and spent, by source, such as a boss, a reward or a shop;
  • the world or tower floor you reached, and boss results;
  • a few other counters, such as boss wins.

The developer reads them in Roblox's Creator Hub to see where players get stuck or leave, and to balance prices and rewards. No outside analytics company is involved. How long Roblox keeps these events is up to Roblox.

Reports you send

In the game's settings there is a window for a bug, an idea or a complaint. A report is sent only when you press SEND. With the text you write, the game saves:

  • your account id and username;
  • the kind (bug, idea or complaint) and your text, after Roblox's text filter;
  • the world you stood in and the exact spot, so the developer can go and look;
  • your device type (phone, tablet or PC), your language, your rebirth count and the game version;
  • the time of sending.

Please do not write your real name, address, phone number or any other private information in a report.

In the game, only the developer's own account can open the list of reports, and it shows up to 120 of them. Other players can't see them. The game belongs to a Roblox group, so anyone the group gives access to the game's data stores could reach them too. Up to 300 reports are stored per day, and each one is deleted automatically after 2 years. The game also keeps the times of your latest sends, to limit how many you can send.

Chat and moderation

Chat belongs to Roblox: its filters, its rules and its parental settings decide who can write to you. The game does not record chat. Roblox's text filter also checks house names and the text of reports.

The game limits how often you can repeat some actions, to stop spam and cheating.

Purchases

The game is free to play, with optional purchases for Robux. Roblox handles every purchase: the developer never sees your card, your payment method or your email.

After a purchase the game keeps the numbers Roblox gives it: the receipt numbers of your latest purchases (so a purchase cannot be given twice), whether you bought a one-time pack, and the total of Robux you spent in the game. Roblox's receipts and refund rules apply.

The Top Supporters board in the game shows a player's name, avatar and total of Robux spent to all players, only if you choose it: SETTINGS → SUPPORTERS, off by default, and turning it off removes you from the board. The total stays in your profile either way. The [VIP] tag in chat and the gold Founder title show other players that you have them.

How long it stays, and the reset on 1 November

Profiles not used for 2 years are deleted. This is checked by hand a few times a year, so it can take a little longer. A profile with purchases may stay longer, and you can ask for any profile to be deleted sooner. The 2 years count from the last time the game saved the profile. Reports are deleted automatically after 2 years, and the times of your sends when your profile is deleted. Older leaderboards and house records that the game no longer reads stay in their stores until they are removed.

On 1 November 2026 all saved profiles were reset, so that everyone started again from the beginning. Only a few things were kept: purchase records, redeemed codes and a few marks of invites and rewards. Settings and intro progress were reset too.

Deleting your data and your choices

You can delete your account yourself in the game: SETTINGS → CHARACTER → DELETE ACCOUNT. The game warns you, gives you a day to think it over and asks once more. Then it deletes your progress, reports, leaderboard entries and your place in a house, and everything you bought with Robux inside the game goes too. Game passes stay with your Roblox account. You can also write to support@pegasohlib.com and give your Roblox username or account id; a parent can write for a child.

I cannot take back events already given to Roblox's analytics, or anything on Roblox itself (your account, chat, purchase history). For those, use Roblox's own tools.

If you ask Roblox to erase your data, Roblox passes the request on to the game's owner, and the same data is deleted within 30 days.

Without deleting anything you can stop all of this by leaving the game: it then saves nothing new.

Children and parents

Many players are children, and Roblox decides who may play, chat and spend. The game does not know your age, does not ask for personal details and does not advertise. The only text a player can send to the developer is a report, and only when they choose to.

Parents can use Roblox's parental controls to limit chat, spending and playing time, and can write to the developer to have a child's data deleted.

Roblox and this page

Your Roblox account and all that Roblox itself collects about you are governed by Roblox's privacy policy and its terms, not by this page. This page covers only what the game saves in its data stores and sends to analytics. The website that holds this page has its own short policy: site privacy.

Changes to this policy

The game is still in development and will change. If the game starts to save or send something new, this page will be changed first, and the date at the top will move.
